## Tone rules

- English prose, Russian-inflected small-town texture. No transliteration jokes.
- Every post reads as a different human: distinct punctuation habits, emoji budgets,
  typo patterns. Lyudmila types in ALL CAPS fragments with ellipses…… Timur types in
  lowercase with abbreviations. Rufat writes complete sentences, no emoji. Mom sends
  triple messages and voice-note-length texts. Oleg quotes everyone before replying.
- Dan per §14: warm tier is genuinely likable; confronted he gets *calmer*; reveal line
  is relief, not rage ("…Finally."). Never theatrical.
- Safety per §22: no real doxxing tradecraft; techniques stay fictional-generic;
  no graphic violence; the horror is patience.
